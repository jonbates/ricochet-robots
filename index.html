Ricochet Robots

Slide robots in straight lines -- they never stop until they hit a wall, the edge, or another robot. Once you think you see a solution, bid how many moves it'll take. Other players have 60 seconds to add their own backup bids. Lowest bid goes first; if they can't land it within their count, the next-lowest backup gets a fresh try. First to round wins takes the match.

Ricochet Robots

Slide robots in straight lines -- they never stop until they hit a wall, the edge, or another robot. Get the matching-colored robot onto the target in as few total moves as possible, then submit. The AI then searches for its own solution -- fewer moves wins the round, and if the AI can't find one in time, you win it outright. First to round wins takes the match.
